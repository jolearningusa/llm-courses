AI assistance is bounded

An assistant may propose a patch, input or test. Freeze the contract and separate acceptance from generation.

Keep the prompt, diff, environment and failing controls. Do not relax the oracle merely to make a candidate pass.

Assessment and learning route

L0–L3 establish ABI, completion and ownership. L4–L5 test failure and bounded orderings. L6–L7 assemble qualification and a capstone decision.

Correctness is a gate; model ticks are never reported as measured hardware milliseconds.
