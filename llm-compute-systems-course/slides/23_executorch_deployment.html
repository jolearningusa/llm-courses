DSP and NPU targets bring their own compilers

  • Qualcomm: the Qualcomm AI Engine Direct (QNN) partitioner hands supported subgraphs to the HTP (Hexagon) or GPU backend. Lowering needs the Qualcomm SDK on the host, and custom operators are C++ HTP op packages.
  • Cadence HiFi, Fusion and Vision: no delegate. Custom operators and compiler passes call NNLIB kernels, 8-bit quantized, and the runtime is built with the Xtensa toolchain.
  • Neither uses Triton. The kernels are vendor libraries, and the vendor SDK is part of the build contract.
