Gradient scaling

FP16 underflow can require gradient scaling and overflow detection. BF16 has a wider exponent range; CPU BF16 autocast commonly uses no GradScaler. Neither guarantees convergence.

Post-training quantization

Frozen model → representative calibration → quantized artifact → held-out evaluation

Do not tune scales and report quality on the same samples. Preserve a fallback and metadata describing the recipe.

9 / 16

Quantization-aware training

Training with simulated / quantized effects → adaptation → conversion → evaluation

QAT incurs training cost and has conversion/backend requirements. A successful fake-quantization forward does not prove the deployed kernel is accelerated.
