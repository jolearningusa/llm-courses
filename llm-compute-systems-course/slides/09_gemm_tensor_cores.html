10 / 19

Layer dimensions meet tile and wave quantization

Tile quantization: a dimension that is not a multiple of the block tile leaves edge tiles doing full-tile math on partial data. Wave quantization: the tile count is rounded up to whole waves of SMs, so a small last wave leaves most SMs idle.

  • cuBLAS runs unaligned shapes, but Tensor Cores are more efficient when M, N and K are multiples of 16 bytes (8 FP16 elements); on A100, multiples of 64 elements.
  • GPT-3 2.7B uses h = 2560 with 32 heads, so h/a = 80. With 20 heads (h/a = 128) one layer ran 1.18× faster in Anthony et al.; padding nanoGPT’s vocabulary from 50,257 to 50,304 is their other example.

The “right multiple” is per target: TPU MXUs are 128×128 before v6e and 256×256 on v6e and TPU7x. Check the target’s matrix instruction or array shape; do not copy a GPU rule to another chip.
