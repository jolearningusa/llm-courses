Initialization and reproducibility

  • Same initial parameters, optimizer and data coverage.
  • Control random seeds and stochastic layers.
  • Record sampling, dropped/padded examples and token weighting.
  • Compare update tensors before interpreting throughput.

Failure modes

A missing rank, inconsistent collective order or uneven loop termination can hang the job. Fail with diagnostics and timeouts; do not mask a protocol error with a larger timeout.
