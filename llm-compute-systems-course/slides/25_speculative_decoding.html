Acceptance rule

a(x) = min(1, p(x)/q(x)), x sampled from q

A zero-probability draft token is not sampled. Rejection needs a correction distribution; silently sampling from p again is generally wrong.

Correction rule

r(x) ∝ max(p(x) − q(x), 0)

The accepted mass plus rejected-and-corrected mass reconstructs the target categorical distribution.
