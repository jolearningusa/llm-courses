The logical cache becomes one quarter as large under the stated formula. Full-attention connectivity is unchanged, so reading retained context remains necessary. Clarify that the exercise compares architecture configurations, not an automatic conversion of arbitrary trained weights.

Worked KV budget

2 × L × Hkv × D × b × Σtokens

32 layers × 8 KV heads × 128 dimensions × BF16 gives 128 KiB per retained token. At 32,768 tokens: 4 GiB logical KV for one sequence.

Add allocator slack, metadata, workspace and weights; do not apply this formula unchanged to MLA.

10 / 16

Expand the factor two into K and V, and BF16 into two bytes. Compute 2×32×8×128×2 = 131,072 bytes per retained token; multiplying by 32,768 gives 4 GiB. This is logical KV for one sequence, with no allocator or workspace overhead.

11 / 16

Ask students to identify the object each optimization changes. FlashAttention targets intermediate traffic, PagedAttention manages state allocation/sharing, and windowing changes attended pairs. These mechanisms can coexist and do not have interchangeable quality contracts.

Compressed and recurrent state

MLA uses an architecture-specific cached representation. State-space models use a different recurrence. Their budgets must follow the actual tensors and positional/state rules.

A smaller representation is useful only when supported kernels and acceptable quality accompany it.
