Worked example: does the KV cache fit?

2 (K, V) × 8 KV heads × 128 × 32 layers × 2 bytes = 131,072 bytes per token

DesignPer token32K × 8 sequences
MHA, 32 KV heads512 KiB128 GiB
GQA, 8 KV heads128 KiB32 GiB
MLA-style latent, 512 + 64 values36 KiB9 GiB

With 16 GB of BF16 weights, MHA does not fit in 80GB at this batch; GQA leaves about 30 GB for activations and overhead. That arithmetic, not taste, is why GQA and MLA exist (decks 13, 16).

What to hand in

  1. A config.json for your model, readable by lab L0, with its parameter count, KV bytes per token and training FLOPs.
  2. A one-page design table: each decision, the option chosen, the deck or paper that supports it, and the alternative you rejected.
  3. One risk you would test first with a small ablation (as in lab L6) before training at full size.
  4. A 10-minute presentation in week 15.

Questions that reviewers will ask

  • Why this many tokens per parameter, given how the model will be served? (decks 06, 28)
  • Would MoE give the same quality for less compute per token, and can your hardware hold all experts? (decks 21, 29)
  • How does an image enter, and how many tokens of context does it cost? (decks 32, 36)
  • What keeps training stable at your scale? (decks 17, 30, 33, 37)
  • Which claim in your design rests on a vendor blog rather than a paper with ablations?
