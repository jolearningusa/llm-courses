Problem: the FFN is most of the model

  • In each block the FFN has 2 × d × dff weights with dff = 4d: twice the attention projections (4 × d × d). About two thirds of a dense Transformer's parameters are FFN.
  • Since 2017 the FFN was max(0, xW1)W2 (ReLU), or GELU in GPT and BERT. Little else had been tried.
  • Gated linear units (Dauphin et al., 2016) had helped convolutional language models. Do gated FFNs help Transformers at equal cost?

Change: a gated FFN

FFNSwiGLU(x) = (Swish(xW) ⊗ xV) W2

  • Variants differ only in the gate's activation: GLU (sigmoid), ReGLU (ReLU), GEGLU (GELU), SwiGLU (Swish, x·σ(x)), Bilinear (none).
  • Three weight matrices instead of two, so dff is cut by 2/3 to keep parameters and FLOPs equal: 3,072 → 2,048 in the experiments.

Evidence: fine-tuning

  • GLUE average (dev): every gated variant beats ReLU (83.80); ReGLU is highest at 84.67, SwiGLU 84.36.
  • SuperGLUE average (dev): SwiGLU is highest at 74.56, against 72.76 for ReLU.
  • The author notes the fine-tuning results are noisy; the gated variants are best on most tasks.
  • Setup: encoder-decoder T5 base (12 + 12 layers, d = 768), span corruption on C4, no dropout in pre-training.

No explanation offered

The paper's conclusion states plainly that it offers no explanation for why these architectures seem to work, and attributes their success "to divine benevolence".

  • One common reading: the gate makes the FFN a product of two linear functions of x, a multiplicative interaction a single activation cannot express.
  • The result was empirical, at one scale, on one model family. It held up because later labs kept finding the same gain.

Afterlife

  • PaLM and LLaMA adopted SwiGLU; LLaMA uses a hidden size of 2/3·4d instead of PaLM's 4d.
  • Gemma uses GeGLU, the other top variant in the paper's Table 1.
  • MoE experts (Part V) are gated FFNs: DeepSeek, Mixtral, Qwen MoE and gpt-oss experts are SwiGLU blocks, gpt-oss with clamping and a residual connection inside the activation.
  • Lab L6 trains a ReLU FFN against a parameter-matched SwiGLU FFN.
