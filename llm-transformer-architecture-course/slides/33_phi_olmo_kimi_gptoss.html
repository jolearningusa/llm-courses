OLMo: everything released, not only weights

  • 1B and 7B models trained on at least 2T tokens (7B: 2.46T), under Apache 2.0.
  • Block: no bias terms, non-parametric layer norm (no learned scale or shift), SwiGLU with hidden 11,008 (about 8/3 · d), RoPE, vocabulary 50,280 padded to 50,304, sequence length 2,048; tied embeddings only for 1B.
  • The 7B model was trained on both AMD MI250X (LUMI) and NVIDIA A100 (MosaicML) clusters with nearly the same results.
  • Why it matters: with the data and checkpoints, researchers can study how capabilities appear during training and what the data contained.

Kimi K2: MuonClip and a 15.5T-token run without spikes

  • The Muon optimizer is more token-efficient than AdamW, but at scale attention logits exploded: some exceeded 1,000 with vanilla Muon.
  • QK-Clip: after each step, if a head's maximum logit is above τ = 100, rescale that head's query and key weights to bring it back. Unlike QK-norm it works with MLA, whose keys are not fully materialized.
  • Result: 15.5T tokens of pre-training with zero loss spikes; YaRN later extends the context to 128K.
  • Post-training targets agents: large-scale synthetic tool-use data and RL. SWE-bench Verified 65.8 (single attempt, agentic).

gpt-oss: attention and format

  • Layers alternate between banded attention with a 128-token window and dense attention (compare Gemma 3, deck 32).
  • 64 query heads of dimension 64 and 8 KV heads (GQA). Each head has a learned bias in the softmax denominator, an attention sink that lets a head attend to nothing (deck 15).
  • RoPE with YaRN extends dense layers to 131,072 tokens.
  • Tokenizer o200k_harmony, 201,088 tokens; the "harmony" chat format separates analysis, commentary and final channels (deck 11).
  • Reasoning effort low, medium or high is set in the system prompt. Released under Apache 2.0.

Modeling impact

  • Data is architecture. Phi-3 shows that at a fixed size, filtering and synthetic data move quality more than block changes.
  • Openness has levels. Weights alone (Llama, Gemma) let you use a model; OLMo's data, code and checkpoints let you study how it was made.
  • The optimizer is part of the design. Kimi K2's QK-Clip exists because Muon changed how logits grow; architecture and optimizer were tuned together.
  • Quantization format shapes the model. gpt-oss ships its MoE weights in MXFP4, and the two sizes are set by what fits in 80GB and 16GB.
  • The convergent block. Pre-norm RMSNorm, SwiGLU, RoPE, GQA or MLA, and fine-grained MoE appear in almost every 2025 family; the differences are in data, scale and training.
