DDPM and latent diffusion

Ho et al. (2020) turned diffusion into a simple recipe: add Gaussian noise in 1,000 steps, train a network to predict that noise, and run the chain backwards to sample. Rombach et al. (2021) moved the process into the latent space of an autoencoder and added cross-attention for text, which became Stable Diffusion.

Problem: a generative model that is stable and covers the data

  • In 2020, GANs gave sharp images but trained unstably and could drop modes; likelihood models (autoregressive pixels, flows) were stable but slower or lower in quality.
  • Diffusion models had been proposed in 2015 but had not produced high-quality samples.
  • An autoregressive Transformer over pixels (decks 02, 37) needs one step per pixel or token; diffusion refines the whole image at every step.

DDPM: the forward and reverse processes

  • Forward: q(x_t | x_{t−1}) = N(√(1−β_t)·x_{t−1}, β_t·I), with β rising linearly from 10⁻⁴ to 0.02 over T = 1,000 steps.
  • Closed form: x_t can be sampled from x₀ in one step, with ᾱ_t the product of (1 − β_s) up to t.
  • Reverse: a network ε_θ(x_t, t) predicts the noise; sampling starts from pure noise and removes a little at each of the 1,000 steps.
Ho et al. 2020, arXiv:2006.11239 §2, §4, Eqs. 2, 4 · bibliography

DDPM: the simple loss

L_simple = Et, x₀, ε ‖ ε − ε_θ(√ᾱ_t·x₀ + √(1−ᾱ_t)·ε, t) ‖²

  • Pick a random step t, noise a training image to that level, and regress the noise. Each step is an ordinary supervised problem.
  • This weighting of the variational bound resembles denoising score matching over many noise levels, and the sampler resembles Langevin dynamics.
  • Network: a U-Net (PixelCNN++ style, group norm), self-attention at the 16 × 16 resolution, and t given as a Transformer sinusoidal embedding (deck 01).
  • Result: CIFAR-10 FID 3.17 and Inception score 9.46 with a 35.7M-parameter model, a new state of the art for unconditional generation.

Latent diffusion: why leave pixel space

  • Pixel-space diffusion models took 150 to 1,000 V100 days to train, and producing 50K samples took about 5 days on an A100.
  • Much of that compute models imperceptible high-frequency detail.
  • Split the job: an autoencoder handles perceptual detail once; the diffusion model works on a small latent that keeps the semantics.
Rombach et al. 2021, arXiv:2112.10752 §1 · bibliography

Latent diffusion: the design

The autoencoder is trained with a perceptual loss and a patch-based adversarial loss, with a light KL or VQ regularizer. f = 4 to 8 gave the best balance: f = 1 trains slowly, f = 32 loses quality.

Modeling impact

  • A different generative paradigm. Instead of predicting the next token, the model predicts noise on all positions at once and is run many times.
  • Attention enters through conditioning. Cross-attention from image features to text tokens is the same mechanism as the 2017 encoder-decoder (deck 01).
  • Compression first, then generation. An autoencoder (or tokenizer) plus a generative model on its codes is the template for DiT (deck 39), Chameleon's image tokens (deck 37) and audio LMs (deck 42).
  • Stable Diffusion was built from this paper and released openly, which made text-to-image a commodity.
