Problem: labeled data is scarce

  • In 2018 most NLP systems were trained per task on labeled data, with pre-trained word vectors as the only shared part.
  • Unlabeled text is plentiful, but it was unclear which objective transfers best and how to hand a pre-trained model to a new task without a new architecture for each one.
  • GPT-1 asks: does one language-modeling objective, followed by light fine-tuning, transfer to classification, entailment, similarity and question answering?

GPT-1 change: decoder-only, then fine-tune

L₁(U) = Σi log P(ui | ui−k, …, ui−1; Θ)

  • A 12-layer Transformer decoder with masked self-attention and no encoder or cross-attention: d = 768, 12 heads, FFN 3072, GELU, learned position embeddings.
  • Pre-trained on BooksCorpus (over 7,000 unpublished books) in 512-token contiguous sequences, with a BPE vocabulary of 40,000 merges.
  • Fine-tuning adds one linear layer and keeps the LM loss as an auxiliary term with weight λ = 0.5.

GPT-1: every task becomes one token sequence

This is the first "context construction" in the course: the task is expressed by how the input is laid out with special tokens, not by new layers. Chat templates (deck 11) are its descendant.

GPT-1 evidence

  • New state of the art on 9 of the 12 datasets studied, including +8.9 points on Story Cloze and +5.7 on RACE.
  • GLUE score 72.8, against a previous best of 68.9.
  • Transferring more pre-trained layers helped steadily; the zero-shot behavior of the pre-trained model improved during pre-training, a hint of what GPT-2 would test directly.
  • Recipe: Adam, maximum learning rate 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ with 2,000 warm-up updates and cosine decay, batch 64, 100 epochs.

GPT-2 question: can the LM do tasks with no fine-tuning?

  • Framing: a general system should model p(output | input, task). If the task can be written as text, a language model trained on enough diverse text may learn to follow it.
  • Data: WebText, pages linked from Reddit posts with at least 3 karma. About 8 million documents, 40 GB, with Wikipedia removed.
  • No task-specific training at all: tasks are posed as prompts (for summarization, the article followed by "TL;DR:").

Modeling impact

  • The decoder-only template. One causal stack, one next-token loss, one output head tied to the input embedding. Every LLM in Part VII has this shape.
  • Tasks as text. GPT-1 encoded tasks with delimiters and fine-tuned; GPT-2 dropped fine-tuning and wrote tasks as prompts. GPT-3 (deck 05) makes this in-context learning.
  • Pre-LN and scaled init are what let depth grow from 12 to 48 layers.
  • Cost shape. Generation is sequential again at inference, one token per forward pass, with a KV cache per layer.

Afterlife and check yourself

  • Learned absolute positions limited GPT-2 to 1024 tokens; RoPE replaced them in Llama and most open models (deck 08).
  • The 50,257-token byte-level BPE vocabulary was reused by GPT-3 and many open models; vocabularies have since grown to 128K and more (deck 07).
  • GELU gave way to SwiGLU (deck 18); LayerNorm to RMSNorm (deck 17).
