Problem: neither pure design is enough

  • Transformers: strong in-context learning, but the KV cache and attention cost grow with context; long contexts do not fit on one GPU.
  • Mamba (deck 23): constant memory and fast generation, but weaker at retrieving and copying from the context.
  • Question: how few attention layers does a mostly-Mamba model need, and can MoE add capacity without adding active compute?
Lieber et al. 2024, arXiv:2403.19887 §1–2 · bibliography

Change: the Jamba block

  • Each block has 8 layers: 1 attention layer and 7 Mamba layers. Every second layer's MLP is an MoE with 16 experts, top-2.
  • The attention layers use GQA; the Mamba layers get extra RMSNorms inside for stability (slide 7).

Only one layer in eight keeps a KV cache, so the cache is 8× smaller than an all-attention model of the same shape. The model fits on a single 80 GB GPU with int8 weights, processing up to 140K tokens.

Evidence: throughput and context

  • At 128K context on 4 A100s, Jamba's throughput is 3× Mixtral's.
  • On one 80 GB GPU it fits 2× Mixtral's context and 7× Llama-2-70B's.
  • The released model supports 256K tokens; training went up to 1M.
  • On standard benchmarks it is comparable to Mixtral 8x7B, which has similar active parameters.

Two surprises

  • No positional encoding needed. Jamba performs about the same with RoPE and with no explicit positions at all; the Mamba layers appear to provide position implicitly, since a recurrence is ordered by construction.
  • Stability needed norms. At 7B, loss spikes came from large activations inside the Mamba layers; adding RMSNorm to those internal activations fixed them.
Lieber et al. 2024, arXiv:2403.19887 §6.4–6.5, Table 8, Figure 9 · bibliography

Modeling impact and afterlife

  • A few attention layers carry retrieval. One in eight was enough here; the rest of the stack can be cheap recurrent layers. Gemma's local and global layers (deck 15) apply the same idea within attention.
  • Three axes at once. Jamba combines a cheap sequence mixer, sparse FFNs and a little attention: memory, active compute and quality are tuned separately.
  • Afterlife. Hybrids became the main way recurrent layers reach production. Qwen3-Next (deck 25) uses the same pattern with Gated DeltaNet in place of Mamba, at a 3:1 ratio.
